# Gemini Logo Prompt — Saul's Podship

## Primary prompt (full logo: mark + wordmark)

> Create a logo for **Saul's Podship**, a biblical teaching ministry whose
> website is www.saulspodship.com — a "ship" carrying the whole Bible, story by
> story. The brand mood is an illuminated manuscript at night: deep wine and
> aged gold on parchment.
>
> **Mark:** a minimal front-view of a small wooden ark/ship whose hull doubles
> as an open book seen from the side; a slender single-line cross rises from
> the hull like a mast, and a soft beam of light (a reference to Saul's
> Damascus-road encounter) descends from above onto the open pages. Elegant,
> symmetric, flat vector, thin consistent 2 px-style gold strokes, no shading.
>
> **Wordmark:** the words "SAUL'S PODSHIP" in all-caps under the mark, set in a
> classical old-style serif (EB Garamond / Merriweather feel), generous
> letter-spacing, in antique gold.
>
> **Palette (use exactly these colors):** background deep wine-black #1A0812;
> primary strokes and text antique gold #D4AF37 with lighter gold #E8C96A
> accents; a whisper of parchment cream #F8F4E3 for the light beam and page
> interiors. No other hues — no blue, no green, no red.
>
> **Craft rules:** flat vector logo, centered on a square canvas, works at 64 px
> and at 6 m; no photorealism, no 3D, no drop shadows, no gradients except a
> faint gold sheen; absolutely no taglines, no extra text, no watermark; crisp
> negative space; suitable for a favicon and a YouTube profile picture.
>
> Also show a second, horizontal lockup: mark on the left, "Saul's Podship"
> serif wordmark beside it, same palette.

## Short version (if the long one over-renders)

> Flat vector logo on #1A0812 wine-black background: a minimalist antique-gold
> (#D4AF37) line-emblem of an open book whose bottom page forms a small ark
> hull, with a thin cross as a mast and one cream-gold light beam falling on
> the pages. Below it the all-caps serif wordmark "SAUL'S PODSHIP" in gold
> #E8C96A. Illuminated-manuscript mood, thin even strokes, symmetric, no
> gradients, no extra text, no other colors.

## Variations you can try after the first result

- **Monogram:** "Turn the letters S and P into a single continuous gold
  line-symbol, S forming a wave/scroll, P forming a mast — same palette,
  favicon-ready."
- **Dark/light pair:** "Produce two squares side by side: version A gold mark on
  wine #1A0812; version B wine mark on parchment #F8F4E3."
- **Icon only (for the favicon):** "Extract just the ark-book-cross emblem, no
  wordmark, square, clear at 16 px."

Negative prompt (add if Gemini's UI supports one):
> photorealistic, 3D render, wood texture, water, sky, clouds, blue, green, red
> accents, glow effects, lens flare, ornate baroque frame, taglines, subtitles,
> watermarks, drop shadow, gradient background.

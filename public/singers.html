Historic Pioneers & Patriarchs of Pakistani Gospel

Figures who laid the foundational identity of Christian/gospel music in the Punjab and Sindh regions before 1950. Their works continue in churches today.

Imam-ud-Din Shahbaz
1845 – 1921
The undisputed pioneer of Pakistani gospel music heritage. A Punjabi evangelist and poet from Zafarwal, he produced the first metrical translation of the entire Book of Psalms into Punjabi, known as Punjabi Zabur, completing all 150 psalms set to local ragas between 1888 and 1908. Earned a Doctor of Divinity from Tarkio College, Missouri. His translations are still sung in Pakistani churches today, making him arguably the single most enduring influence in Pakistani Christian worship music. Died 1921 in Bhalwal, Pakistan.
Saleem Raza (Noel Dias)
1932 – 1983
Born Noel Dias to a Christian family in Amritsar. A classically trained singer who converted to Islam and became one of Pakistan's greatest film playback singers. Originally sang gospel music before entering the Lollywood circuit where he earned two Nigar Awards (1960, 1963). Died November 25, 1983 in Vancouver, Canada.
Robin Ghosh
1939 – 2016
Pakistani-Bangladeshi playback singer and film music composer. Born in Baghdad to a Bengali father and Arab Catholic mother; first developed his musical talent singing in church choirs. Known as the "Madan Mohan of Pakistan," he composed music for landmark Lollywood films including Chanda, Talash, Chakori, Aaina. Won 6 Nigar Awards. Died February 13, 2016 in Dhaka, Bangladesh, and was buried with Christian rites at the Old Christian Cemetery, Wari.
Note on Irene Parveen: Born 1940, she sang gospel-inflected film songs in Pakistani cinema through the 1960s alongside Ahmed Rushdi and Masood Rana. She migrated to the UK in the early 1980s. Her present status is not publicly confirmed; she is noted as a veteran of significant historic importance.
Deceased Gospel Legends — Rest in Glory

Beloved gospel artists whose voices and ministries have passed on but whose recorded music remains a living legacy.

✝ Deceased
S. B. John
Sunny Benjamin John
Gospel Singer / Ghazal Vocalist / PTV Christmas Tradition
Born: ~1930, Karachi · Died: June 5, 2021 (aged ~90), Karachi
Popularly known as S. B. John, this legendary singer from Karachi began his career at Radio Pakistan in 1950. From 1967 onward he became the beloved face of Pakistani Christmas television, singing gospel on PTV every Christmas Eve alongside his wife and sons Robin and Donald John. Received the President's Pride of Performance Award in 2010/2011. Sons Robin and Donald pursued music careers in America.

Pastor Ernest Mall
Rev. Dr. Ernest Mall
Gospel Singer / Songwriter / Evangelist
Home-going anniversary observed July 14 each year (approx. died ~2010s)
One of the most beloved gospel voices in Pakistani Christian history. Transitioned from secular to sacred music in 1986, dedicating his gifts entirely to spreading the message of the Cross. Globally recognized, he wrote lyrics for countless Christian songs and ghazals in Urdu and Punjabi based on scripture. His official Facebook page is maintained by his wife and daughter.

Established senior gospel artists whose decades of service have shaped Pakistani Christian music. Some are retired, some still active in limited capacities.

Veteran
A. Nayyar
Arthur Nayyer · Arthur Nayyar
Renowned Playback Singer / Gospel Circuit (Diaspora)
One of Pakistan's most celebrated playback singers, member of Lahore's large Christian community. Credited with over 5,000 film songs, with a voice compared to Kishore Kumar — strong, masculine and joyful. Debuted 1974 and became one of the biggest Lollywood stars of the 1980s alongside Nadeem and Babra Sharif. Now sings primarily on the gospel music circuit in American and European churches.

Leo Twins
Haroon Leo · Sharoon Leo
Multi-Instrumentalists / Gospel & Fusion / Songs of Worship
Born July 27, 1993 in Rawalpindi to a Christian family — Haroon and Sharoon Leo are Pakistan's most celebrated musical twins. Raised in the church singing Punjabi and Urdu hymns, psalms, and carols, they rose to national fame through Nescafé Basement Season 3. Sharoon plays violin and cello; Haroon masters over 10 instruments including guitar, rubab, tabla, piano, cajon, and mandolin. Their gospel output is profound: their first studio recording was a reimagined psalm blending guitar and violin for younger audiences. They actively release Pakistani Christian worship content under their "Songs of Worship" gospel platform alongside their mainstream career. Have collaborated with Hammad Baily and other gospel artists. Performed with Atif Aslam, Rahat Fateh Ali Khan, and Momina Mustehsan.

Active · Wikipedia
Leo Twins (Haroon & Sharoon Leo)
Multi-Instrumentalist Gospel Musicians / Rawalpindi
Born July 27, 1993 in Rawalpindi into a Punjabi Christian family, Haroon and Sharoon Leo are among the most celebrated Pakistani Christian musicians of their generation — with a full Wikipedia page. Sharoon plays violin and cello; Haroon masters 10+ instruments including guitar, rubab, tabla, piano, cajon, and darbuka. Rose to national fame on Nescafé Basement (Season 3, 4, 5). Have performed with Atif Aslam, Meesha Shafi, Momina Mustehsan, and Rahat Fateh Ali Khan. Their Christian faith is central to their identity: "In the subcontinent, we worship in our mother tongue. Gospel music for us was in Punjabi and Urdu." Released Psalm (Zaboor) 62 instrumental and a 2023 Christmas Carols Medley.

Veteran · Active
Shallum Xavier
Shallum Asher Xavier
Guitarist / Composer / Music Producer / Fuzon / Gospel Collaborator
One of Pakistan's most acclaimed guitarists and co-founder of the legendary band Fuzon (2003). A Pakistani Christian from Karachi who has recorded professionally for 25+ years, performing with Ali Azmat, Ali Haider, Najam Shiraz, Zeb and Haniya, Hadiqa Kiyani, and Strings. Two-time award winner for Best Guitarist of Pakistan, and won Best South Asian Band and Best Composer for "Khamaj." Collaborated with Ingrid Kindem (Norway) and has performed internationally. Frequently collaborates on gospel recordings with Pakistani Christian artists including Faraz Nayyer ("Mera Yaar") and Aamir Karamat ("Javaan Bhalla"). His Christian faith is woven through his musical collaborations.
